Where do these jobs come from?

NextRole checks its connected employer APIs and public feeds centrally. This inbox shows up to 30 jobs from your 60 most recent match alerts within 30 days; it is not the entire catalogue. Applied, Interested and Not interested jobs, closed listings and uncertain eligibility are excluded. Background checks run about every 15 minutes while Chrome is running; source collection follows NextRole’s own schedule.

LinkedIn, Unstop and Internshala automatic feeds are not connected. An extension cannot unlock those feeds. Use their own alerts or save an individual job with its original link and pasted description. Scout does not read or crawl those accounts.
